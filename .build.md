## PROJECTS

**CMOS Ring Oscillator Design** | [github.com/AQLYSR/cmos-ring-oscillator](https://github.com/AQLYSR/cmos-ring-oscillator)

- Designed a 5-stage CMOS ring oscillator in XSchem using SkyWater Sky130 (130 nm) PDK, with 1.0 µm × 0.15 µm CMOS inverter device at 1.8 V supply.
- Characterized the CMOS inverter using DC and transient simulations, extracting a 0.833 V switching threshold and 22.11 ps average propagation delay.
- Achieved 3.99 GHz simulated oscillation frequency at the typical process corner, 1.8 V and 27 °C, within 12 % of the 4.52 GHz analytical prediction.
- Performed PVT sensitivity analysis across 1.6-2.0 V, -10-80 °C, and ss/tt/ff process corners, observing ~96%/V voltage sensitivity and a 1.99× frequency spread between slow and fast corners.

**Digital PWM Generator Design** | [github.com/AQLYSR/pwm-generator](https://github.com/AQLYSR/pwm-generator)

- Designed a synthesizable 8-bit PWM generator in Verilog using a counter-comparator architecture, providing 256-step duty-cycle resolution (0-99.6%) with a 390.6 kHz output at 100 MHz clock.
- Developed cocotb functional verification with Icarus Verilog, validating 25% duty-cycle accuracy within ±1% and the 0% duty-cycle boundary case, achieving 2/2 passing tests.
- Implemented complete RTL-to-GDS flow using Librelane and the Sky130A PDK, including synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, and GDS generation.
- Achieved +4.95 ns worst-case setup slack and +0.108 ns hold slack across 9 PVT signoff corners, with 0 timing violations at the 100 MHz target clock.
